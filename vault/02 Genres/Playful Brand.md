# Playful Brand

Best for: Consumer experiences, events, youth-oriented or social brands.

Intent: A memorable identity with dependable controls.

## Page structure

Distinctive brand moment > clear offer > story or examples > task details > action.

## Rules

Use distinctive illustration, colour and copy grounded in the brand. Keep control affordances and labels predictable. Limit decorative movement and preserve focus visibility.

## Professional exception

Emojis can fit an explicitly social or playful voice. Use them sparingly in content rather than substituting ambiguous control labels.

## Avoid

Avoid making every element bounce, unreadable novelty fonts, surprise audio and a visual joke that obscures the main task.

## Mobile and validation

Provide touch-friendly alternatives to hover games. Reduce decorative motion and keep essential text as real text.
