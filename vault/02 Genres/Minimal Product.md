# Minimal Product

Best for: Product marketing, focused software, small tools.

Intent: A clear promise, a genuine product view and a short path to trying it.

## Page structure

Task-led hero > real product demonstration > workflow proof > supported customer evidence > decision details > CTA.

## Rules

One strong sans hierarchy; restrained neutrals; sparse borders; content-led grid. Use product-specific screenshots and exact feature language. Keep a small number of competing actions.

## Professional exception

Centered hero and neutral typography can be appropriate if the real product is distinctive. A bento is justified by unequal content importance, not fashion.

## Avoid

Avoid the generic dark mesh, badge, glowing CTA and three-card stack. Do not put an imaginary dashboard in a pretend browser window.

## Mobile and validation

Stack promise, action and product view in reading order; make previews zoomable or replace them with readable detail crops.

Starting dials: 3-6 / 1-3 / 3-5. These are this library's proposed recipes, not claims about measured conversion. Apply [Core Design Rules](../01%20Foundations/Core%20Design%20Rules.md) and [Accessibility Rules](../06%20Quality/Accessibility%20Rules.md).
