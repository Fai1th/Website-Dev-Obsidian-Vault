# Local Business

Best for: Trades, venues, studios, professional services.

Intent: Help the right visitor understand service area and contact or book.

## Page structure

Service/location promise > real work > service scope > process > verified reviews > contact or booking.

## Rules

Prioritize accurate contact, service area, hours when relevant and real examples. Explain what happens after an enquiry. Use business-specific photos and clear action labels. Keep forms short.

## Professional exception

A real address, local time or map may be relevant here. A familiar service-card grid can help compare offerings.

## Avoid

Avoid fake reviews, irrelevant atmosphere strips, made-up accreditations and stock photos that imply completed projects.

## Mobile and validation

Make contact and booking usable on a phone. Avoid maps or fixed chat widgets that trap scrolling or cover the submit action.
