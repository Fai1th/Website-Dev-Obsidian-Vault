# Design Genres

Choose by audience, task, brand and available content. Dials below are optional variance / motion / density heuristics, each from 0-10; they are not package settings or standards.

- [Minimal Product](Minimal%20Product.md): Product marketing, focused software, small tools.
- [Editorial](Editorial.md): Publications, research, journalism, long-form stories.
- [Swiss Grid](Swiss%20Grid.md): Architecture, cultural programmes, factual brand systems.
- [Premium Commerce](Premium%20Commerce.md): Considered purchases, product brands, high-quality retail.
- [Studio Portfolio](Studio%20Portfolio.md): Designers, agencies, photographers, creative teams.
- [Enterprise Dashboard](Enterprise%20Dashboard.md): Business applications, admin tools, analytics.
- [Public Service](Public%20Service.md): Government, civic services, complex applications.
- [Local Business](Local%20Business.md): Trades, venues, studios, professional services.
- [Playful Brand](Playful%20Brand.md): Consumer experiences, events, youth-oriented or social brands.
- [Experimental Campaign](Experimental%20Campaign.md): Short-lived art, culture, launch and campaign experiences.
- [Dark Developer](Dark%20Developer.md): Developer tools, documentation, technical products.
