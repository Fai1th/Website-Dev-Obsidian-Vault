# Experimental Campaign

Best for: Short-lived art, culture, launch and campaign experiences.

Intent: An expressive concept people can still understand and navigate.

## Page structure

Concept-led opening > narrative sequence > supporting context > clear destination/action.

## Rules

Choose one strong compositional idea. Define the accessible/static version first. Budget media and motion. Keep navigation, headings and exit routes available. Explain unusual interactions.

## Professional exception

Kinetic typography, immersive media or scroll choreography can fit this brief when the actual story requires it.

## Avoid

Avoid scroll hijacking, custom cursors that replace normal input, inaccessible WebGL-only text and cinematic delays before usable content.

## Mobile and validation

Use a linear narrative fallback. Test reduced motion, slow connections, keyboard-only navigation and low-powered phones.
