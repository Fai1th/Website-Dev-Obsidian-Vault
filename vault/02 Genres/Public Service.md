# Public Service

Best for: Government, civic services, complex applications.

Intent: People completing important tasks with minimal ambiguity.

## Page structure

Service title > eligibility > requirements > start > focused steps > review > confirmation.

## Rules

Use the relevant established public design system when appropriate. Write plain labels, visible requirements, helpful errors and confirmation details. Preserve form data across recoverable navigation. Use familiar controls.

## Professional exception

A conventional layout is a strength when it reduces risk and cognitive load. An organizational identity does not establish a legal obligation to use a named package.

## Avoid

Avoid novelty navigation, automatic motion, inaccessible validation, forced timeouts without explanation and decorative trust seals.

## Mobile and validation

Single-column forms, visible actions, robust zoom and no reliance on hover or precise pointer movement.
