# Premium Commerce

Best for: Considered purchases, product brands, high-quality retail.

Intent: Confidence through product detail, photography and a legible purchase path.

## Page structure

Product-led hero > benefits with evidence > detail gallery > specifications > delivery/returns > purchase.

## Rules

Build around the actual material, product and brand. Show useful scale, dimensions, variants, price and availability. Keep selections and purchase state clear. Provide honest delivery/return information before commitment.

## Professional exception

Warm cream, serif or purple can work when already part of the identity. Do not pick beige and brass solely because the product is called premium.

## Avoid

Avoid invented craftsmanship stories, fake stock counters, decorative specifications and luxury styling that hides the price.

## Mobile and validation

Place variant, price and purchase action together. Sticky purchase bars must not cover content, keyboard focus or error feedback.

Starting dials: 5-8 / 1-4 / 3-5. These are this library's proposed recipes, not claims about measured conversion. Apply [Core Design Rules](../01%20Foundations/Core%20Design%20Rules.md) and [Accessibility Rules](../06%20Quality/Accessibility%20Rules.md).
