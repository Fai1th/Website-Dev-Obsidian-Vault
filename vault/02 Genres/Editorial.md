# Editorial

Best for: Publications, research, journalism, long-form stories.

Intent: Reading and discovery supported by a distinct editorial hierarchy.

## Page structure

Masthead > lead story > varied story grid > topic collections > article > related reading.

## Rules

Use title, deck, byline, date and body roles consistently. Measure body lines; prioritize reading comfort. Use strong crops and varied lead/support hierarchy. Keep article navigation and attribution clear.

## Professional exception

Serif is justified for an actual editorial identity when the chosen face fits. Text-first articles do not need a decorative hero photo.

## Avoid

Avoid fake issue numbers, poetic labels without meaning, unnecessary giant pull quotes and long low-contrast paragraphs.

## Mobile and validation

Collapse story grids by editorial priority. Keep body measure comfortable and media captions attached to their images.
