# Studio Portfolio

Best for: Designers, agencies, photographers, creative teams.

Intent: Distinctive work and credible case studies.

## Page structure

Selected work > concise positioning > case studies > capabilities tied to outcomes > contact.

## Rules

Let real work lead. Use project images with purposeful scale and concise role/outcome captions. Keep services and navigation straightforward. Explain constraints and decisions inside case studies.

## Professional exception

Expressive type, unusual compositions and more motion may fit a creative studio. Provide a quiet reading path and ordinary contact behavior.

## Avoid

Avoid fake client logos, meaningless city clocks, long creative manifestos and identical contact labels with different wording.

## Mobile and validation

Keep project cover images understandable without hover. Make filters and pagination reachable and preserve back-navigation state.
