# Page Structures

Use page families rather than one landing-page template everywhere.

| Family | Structure | Critical decision |
|---|---|---|
| Service | offer > scope > evidence > process > contact | Is the visitor qualified and ready to contact? |
| Product marketing | promise > product proof > workflow > fit/pricing > action | Can the product demonstrate its claim? |
| Product detail | gallery > price/variants > specifications > delivery/returns > purchase | Is material purchase information visible? |
| Portfolio | selected work > case studies > practice > contact | Does the work support the positioning? |
| Article | title/deck > attribution/date > body/media > related reading | Is sustained reading comfortable? |
| Search/catalogue | query > filters > count/results > sort/pagination > empty state | Are criteria and current state recoverable? |
| Workspace | shell > task header > filters > records > details | Is the next operation obvious? |
| Transaction | requirements > focused steps > review > confirmation | Can errors recover without losing work? |

For each section, record its user question, content, action, layout family, mobile fallback and evidence. Delete unsupported filler. A layout is a candidate until real content fits it. Genre notes supply visual direction, not a mandatory section count.
